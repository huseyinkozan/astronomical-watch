# Tools and Observing pages: implementation plan (2026-11-21)

The owner approved the tool set and the mock-ups and asked for all of it to be built without waiting for feedback;
he tests by hand at the end. Decisions and sources: `research/watch-tools.md` (both "Owner's decisions" sections),
`research/tools-by-domain.md`; mock-ups in `research/mockups/` (`tools-sheet.png`, `tools-sheet-2.png`,
`tools-sheet-3.png`, `tools-sheet-4.png`; `hit-zones.png` for the face). Both `research/` folders are git-excluded:
read them, do not commit them.

## Global rules
- CLAUDE.md applies in full (code style, KISS, EN/TR texts from `main/Language.*` short enough for their rows,
  fonts 26/48 px only — a new glyph goes into `tools/make_fonts.sh` and the C files are made again, or draw a small
  icon with lines; LVGL printf has no `%f`; no call with an effect inside an `ESP_LOGx` argument; battery first:
  tools run only in active mode, sensors only while their part is shown).
- Astronomy and pure computations live in host-testable modules (plain C++, no ESP-IDF/LVGL) with host tests in
  `tests/` added to `tests/run_host_tests.sh`; astronomical reference values come from Skyfield
  (`tools/.venv`, `tools/make_*_reference.py` style) where a computation is new; a tolerance needs a reason.
- Memory: the launcher builds one stacked page at a time; keep parts cheap (shared styles, `TablePart` where it fits),
  no heap growth that starves the portal's Wi-Fi (check the "Display started ... internal heap" line).
- Each stage on branch `tools` (from `master`), small commits whose messages say what was verified, ending with
  "Co-Authored-By: Claude Opus 5.5 <noreply@anthropic.com>". No merge, push, tag or release.
- Board: only /dev/serial/by-id/usb-Espressif_USB_JTAG_serial_debug_unit_3C:DC:75:6E:F4:54-if00; never another
  device; no serial logger while flashing (kill "ota/log.py" processes of the main session first). The owner is
  away: no PWR presses; flash at an always-on minute wake by catching the port and resetting with RTS (as earlier
  agents did). Screenshots with `tools/screenshot.py` into a scratch folder (not docs/).
- Update CLAUDE.md (pages, parts, development commands) and docs in the same change.

## Structure
- Two new stacked pages in the launcher (`s_pageEntries`), each at most 8 parts, orderable/hideable in the portal
  like the others (new Settings items for order and hidden parts; portal orderLists; `tools/portal_preview.py`):
  - **Tools** ("Tools" / "Araçlar"), after Info: 1 Torch, 2 Calculator, 3 Unit converter, 4 Spirit level,
    5 Counter, 6 Random, 7 Metronome, 8 Emergency card.
  - **Observing** ("Observing" / "Gözlem"), after Tools: 1 Polar alignment, 2 Milky Way window, 3 Deep-sky finder,
    4 Eyepiece calculator, 5 Photo calculators, 6 Nautical almanac, 7 Astrolabe, 8 Aiming.
- Hidden by default (portal can show): Metronome, Emergency card (shown once the portal has data for it),
  Nautical almanac, Aiming.
- Face taps (FaceTap) need no change; the launcher lists the new pages automatically.

## Stages (in order; each ends with host tests, build, screenshots, commits)
1. **Pages and simple tools**: the Tools and Observing pages (Observing may start with placeholder-free parts only
   as they are built — do not show empty parts), portal order/hidden for both, then:
   - Torch: the whole screen one colour (red, amber, white); hold + horizontal drag = colour, hold + vertical drag =
     brightness (the panel's brightness while the torch is shown, restored on leaving); a quick vertical swipe still
     steps the parts; a tap restarts a 2 min auto-off shown as a countdown ("Kırmızı · %30 · 1:47"); while the torch
     runs the display timeout does not end active mode; at 0:00 active mode ends as at a timeout. Not converted by
     the night-red mode (like the QR code). SOS mode (Morse SOS flashes, optional beeps at the lowest level) as a
     colour step after white.
   - Counter: big count, "+1" big key, undo key (drawn arrow), target selector (none, 33, 99, 100, and a 3-step
     33/33/33 sequence showing "step 2 / 3"), rate per hour since the first count, long press on the count = reset.
     Count and target survive sleep and power-off (NVS written at active-mode end only, not per tap). BOOT short
     press = +1 while the counter is shown when the new setting "BOOT on counter" is "count" (default "count";
     portal + settings page row if there is room, else portal only); the on-screen back "‹" still goes back.
   - Random: dice (1-3 dice), coin, number 1-100; tap = roll (`esp_random`).
2. **Calculator and unit converter**: a host-tested expression evaluator (precedence, parentheses, unary minus,
   sin cos tan asin acos atan, sqrt, x², x^y, log, ln, 1/x, π, EXP, ±, degrees default / radians toggle, errors
   shown as "Error"/"Hata"). Normal keypad and scientific keypad ("fn" toggles); C and = double width and accented
   (mock-ups). Navigation computations as a third keypad/mode only if simple: speed-time-distance(-fuel) solver;
   leave the wind triangle out if it does not fit the keypad model (note it). Unit converter: categories selected by
   a tap on the category title (vertical swipes stay for the parts): angle/time (h:m:s, degrees, radians),
   distance (km, AU, light year, parsec), temperature (°C, °F, K); value entry with the calculator's keypad
   component; host-tested conversions.
3. **Spirit level and astrolabe**: sample the QMI8658 accelerometer only while the part is shown (power it down
   again on leaving; reuse the Motion module's driver; check docs/hardware.md for its power modes). Level: bubble,
   pitch/roll, the watch edge's altitude angle, a sighting mode (height from distance and angle), a 30-45° slope
   band. Astrolabe (Observing 7): dial mode (stereographic projection for the location: horizon, almucantars,
   ecliptic, the Sun, ~15 bright stars; drag rotates time, shows the time), alidade mode (edge altitude of the Sun
   → local time, the AM/PM solution chosen by the current time; of Polaris → latitude), sundial and analemma views.
   Pure geometry host-tested.
4. **Observing tools**: polar alignment (Polaris' hour angle with precession to date, reticle with clock positions,
   inverted by default, a tap flips), Milky Way window (galactic centre up, astronomical darkness, no Moon; tonight's
   window, its highest time and altitude, the next good night), deep-sky finder (a static Messier table: name,
   RA/Dec, magnitude; listed by altitude now with tonight's best time; scrollable list like the launcher's rows),
   eyepiece calculator (telescope and eyepieces from the portal; magnification, exit pupil, true field, limiting
   magnitude, resolution), photo calculators (500 rule, NPF, ND filter, star-trail frames; inputs from the portal
   or a few presets), nautical almanac (Sun, Moon, planets: GHA, declination, semi-diameter, local noon and its
   altitude), aiming (geostationary dish elevation/azimuth for a chosen satellite longitude, a solar panel tilt,
   magnetic declination from the WMM 2025 model at the location, and "the Sun stands in this direction at HH:MM").
   New astronomy host-tested against Skyfield reference data; WMM against NOAA's published values.
5. **Additions to existing parts**: Sun part: daylight left and a turnaround time (half the daylight left);
   Hijri calendar: kandils and religious days (Diyanet's list: Regaip, Miraç, Berat, Kadir, Mevlid, Ramadan's first
   day, Ramazan and Kurban Bayramı, Hijri new year, Aşure) and a Ramadan countdown; prayer times: kerahat times;
   countdown: a regatta 5-4-1 start mode; Time part: a UTC row. Host-tested date rules.
6. **Portal and help**: emergency card fields, telescope/eyepiece/camera data, the BOOT-on-counter setting, the new
   pages' order/hidden lists with the defaults above, and one help page per tool reached from a tree like the
   launcher's (Tools → Torch …); keep each page small (gzipped); check flash and the portal's heap.

(both languages where cheap; put Turkish back); heap numbers; CLAUDE.md updated. Record measurements in
