Using the watch

← Back to the settings

Buttons and gestures

  • PWR wakes the watch. Hold it for at least half a second when the watch is off. A press while it is on dims it at once. Hold 6 s to switch it off completely.
  • Swipe sideways between the pages: always-on preview, watch face, clock, information, settings.
  • Swipe up or down for the next part of a page, or the next style on the faces. The marks on the right edge show where you are.
  • On the calendars a sideways swipe changes the month (the < > at the bottom say so). Swipe up or down to another part to leave the page.
  • Hold and drag up or down on the watch face to set the brightness; on the always-on page, the always-on brightness of day or night.

Screen corners

Top left: the day (yellow) or the night (blue) passing. Top right: the battery. Bottom left: the Moon above (white) or below (grey) the horizon. Bottom right: the Moon's phase. The notch marks the middle.

Battery

When the screen times out the watch shows a dim always-on face and sleeps. If it lies still for a while, or the battery is at 10 %, it switches off; PWR (half a second) starts it again. On USB the screen can stay on (Settings, "On USB").

Clock page

  • World clock: your time and up to four cities chosen here.
  • Stopwatch and timer keep running while the watch sleeps. The timer gives no signal yet; look at the watch.
  • Days: days until or since the dates you set here.

Time and daylight saving

This page sets the watch from your phone's clock. Choosing your city gives its time zone; the watch then changes for daylight saving time by itself for a year. Open this page again within a year, and whenever you travel to another city: the Sun, Moon and prayer times are for the place set here, which the Sun and prayer pages show at the bottom.

Prayer times

By the method of Turkey's Diyanet (dawn 18°, night 17°, afternoon with a shadow of one length, Diyanet's precaution minutes). Usually equal to Diyanet's tables to the minute. North of 44.5° Diyanet uses special rules; the watch then says "approx.".

Sun compass

Hold the watch flat and turn it until its top points at the Sun. The arrow then points north. It works when the Sun is up and not too high.

Qibla time

At the time shown under "Qibla" (Time page) the Sun stands exactly in the direction of the Kaaba. Stand with the Sun in front of you at that minute: you face the qibla. The degrees next to it are the qibla direction from north.

Other times (Time page)

  • Solar: sundial time, 12:00 when the Sun is highest.
  • Eq. time: how much sundial time is ahead of (+) or behind (−) the mean.
  • Ezani: the old Ottoman time, 12:00 at every sunset.
  • Sidereal: star time; a star returns to the same place at the same sidereal time.

Hijri date

"Local crescent": a month starts after the new crescent can be seen by eye where you are. "Diyanet": Turkey's official calendar.

Wi-Fi setup

Start it on the watch (Settings, swipe up to Wi-Fi setup), join the network shown there with its password, and this page opens. If it does not, open http://192.168.4.1 in the browser. The network closes itself after a few minutes, or when you save.
