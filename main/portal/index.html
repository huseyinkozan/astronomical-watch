Astronomical Watch

Time
The watch takes this device's clock, so it is as accurate as this device. Taken from this device; change it when daylight saving time starts or ends.
Location
Degrees; north and east are positive. A city fills them in.
Watch Local: the new crescent visible to the naked eye at the watch's location (Odeh's criterion). Diyanet: Turkey's official rule (Istanbul 1978: anywhere on land, 8° from the Sun and 5° high at sunset).
