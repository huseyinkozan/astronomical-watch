Astronomical Watch

How to use the watch

Time
The watch takes this device's clock, so it is as accurate as this device.
Location
Degrees; north and east are positive. A city fills them in.
For a place that is not in the list (a district, a village, a mountain): before you join the watch's network, while the phone has internet, open Google Maps or Apple Maps and touch and hold the place; a pin drops there and a card opens at the bottom. Swipe the card up: the coordinates are on a line of their own (like 41.0082, 28.9784); tapping that line copies them. Do not tap the coordinates in the search box at the top: there they turn into an address. Then join the watch and paste them here.
World clock The watch follows their clock changes (daylight saving time) for a year from now; open this page again within a year.
Day counters
Alarms At a clock time or by a sky event (up to four); switched on and off on the watch's clock page. With no day chosen an alarm rings once.
An alarm that is on keeps the watch from switching itself off: the battery lasts shorter.
Corner gauges The gauges in the four screen corners are the same on every page.
Pages The pages of the watch face; on the watch, swipe up or down on the face for the next one. The always-on face shows the page shown when active mode ends: its clock and the items marked "always-on".
Preview
Watch On the watch: hold a finger on the watch face; drag up or down for the face's brightness, sideways for the always-on brightness of the moment. Turned around for a watch worn the other way up, mirrored for a look through a mirror. On the watch: while holding and dragging for the brightness, a PWR press mirrors the picture in that direction. Auto: from civil dusk to civil dawn (the Sun 6° down) everything is shown in red, so that the eye stays used to the dark. The Wi-Fi QR code stays as it is. Diyanet uses 17° in Turkey and 16° in some countries (for example Romania, Bosnia, Canada); see its prayer times for your city. Local: the new crescent visible to the naked eye at the watch's location (Odeh's criterion). Diyanet: Turkey's official rule (Istanbul 1978: anywhere on land, 8° from the Sun and 5° high at sunset).
Pages The order of the pages in the watch's page list; the order of the parts a vertical swipe steps through, and which of them to show.

Update and logs page →

A new version is installed from the browser, not from the phone's sign-in window; the page explains how. The logs for a problem report are there too.
Saving sets the watch and closes its Wi-Fi network; your phone returns to its usual network. To change something later, start the setup on the watch again.
