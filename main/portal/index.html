Astronomical Watch

How to use the watch

Time
The watch takes this device's clock, so it is as accurate as this device.
Location
Degrees; north and east are positive. A city fills them in.
Watch On the watch: hold a finger on the watch face (or on the always-on page for the always-on brightness of the moment) and drag up or down. Local: the new crescent visible to the naked eye at the watch's location (Odeh's criterion). Diyanet: Turkey's official rule (Istanbul 1978: anywhere on land, 8° from the Sun and 5° high at sunset).
World clock The watch follows their clock changes (daylight saving time) for a year from now; open this page again within a year.
Day counters
Faces
On the watch: swipe up or down on the watch face, or on the always-on page left of it. The screen corners show the day or night, the battery, the Moon's time above or below the horizon and its phase.
